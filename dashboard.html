Data notes

  • Counts come from third-party trackers and press coverage, not from platform APIs, so they are rounded and sometimes disagree.
  • Vine follower totals at shutdown range from 9.3M to 9.47M across sources, and sources date the shutdown to 2016 or 2017.
  • 2019 to 2021 has the thinnest coverage: one subscriber snapshot (April 2020) between 2018 and 2022.
  • Sources conflict on when his WWE contract began (2022 or 2023).
  • CryptoZoo litigation entries are allegations and procedural events. Paul has denied wrongdoing, and the Coffeezilla suit was dismissed after a reported settlement.
  • Timing overlaps in this page are correlations. They do not show that one event caused a change in growth.
